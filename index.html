🎤 Between-Track Vibe Checks
Vibe Check
Station
Every great artist takes 3-minute resets between sessions — that's not quitting, that's producing
🎵
🧠
Vibe checks are NOT skipping the routine — they are built into the session. Every top producer takes resets. 3 minutes between tracks gives your nervous system a beat to land on before the next drop.
🌬️
Beat Breath
Breathe in for 4 counts. Hold for 4 counts. Out for 4 counts. Repeat 4 times. That's the drop — feel it.
💃
Freestyle Minute
One minute. No rules. Dance around your room, jump on the spot, or just shake it out. The beat is yours.
🏆
Hype Speech
Look in the mirror and say 3 things you like about yourself out loud. Point to yourself. Own the stage.
🧘
Still Water
Sit completely still for 2 minutes. No phone. No music. Just notice the silence. Let the track breathe.
🎤
Flow State
Rap or sing anything — even gibberish — for 60 seconds. No judging, no editing. Just let the words out.

You are the Executive Producer — calm, hype, and always one track ahead.

Step 1: Print the Artist Pass together — pick their genre.
Step 2: Open the Setlist together — show all 5 tracks to record.
Step 3: Give the 2 FREE spins immediately for showing up.
Step 4: Issue Track 01 only — not all five at once.
